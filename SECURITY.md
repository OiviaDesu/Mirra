# Security reports

Mirra handles SSH access, device certificates and authenticated local
connections. Keep credentials and account information out of public issues.

For a vulnerability, use GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** option when private
reporting is enabled. Otherwise, contact the maintainer through a verified
private channel before sharing sensitive details.

Retain original logs locally and review redacted exports before sending them.
Automatic redaction cannot recognize every kind of private content. Do not
share passwords, private keys, certificate bundles, session tokens or private
connection profiles.

Verify SSH host fingerprints through a trusted source and grant device access
only to people you trust.
